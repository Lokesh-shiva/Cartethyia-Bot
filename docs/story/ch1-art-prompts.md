# Chapter 1 art prompts - "The Cost of Ember"

Use the same style line on every prompt so the set matches your existing character and boss art. For anything with Bren, attach `assets/Characters/Bren.png` as the reference image so his face and outfit stay consistent.

**Style line (paste at the end of every prompt):**
Stylized anime game illustration in the style of Wuthering Waves, painterly shading, dark fantasy, ember-orange and ash-grey palette, cinematic lighting, no text, no watermark.

Sizes: backdrops and scenes 16:9; boss and character art tall 2:3; enemies and weapon square 1:1.

## Scenes (16:9)

1. **Ashen Reach backdrop** - A vast valley of black ash under a dim red sky, cracked ground venting thin lines of glowing orange resonance, jagged ruined towers far away, empty and quiet.
2. **The Furnace Shrine** - A collapsed stone furnace, still warm, a small glowing relic sitting in the coals at the center, nobody present.
3. **The Cooling Pool** - A still pool of black water in a cave of cooled ash, pale moonlight from a hole above, soft ripples, calm and cold.
4. **The Stranger** - A woman in ash-grey hooded robes standing at the edge of the pool, face half hidden, drifting ember fog behind her.
5. **Flashback: The First Scar** - A young, scarred swordsman standing alone in a burning night street, flames behind him, sepia and violet tinted, memory-like soft edges.
6. **Bren POV** - First-person view of a scarred forearm gripping a heavy blade, glowing embers drifting past, a burning valley blurred in the background.

## Characters and enemies

7. **The Last Ember (boss, 2:3)** - A towering humanoid shape made of a dying flame and cracked black stone, a single huge ember burning in its chest like an open furnace, slow and sorrowful, not a monster face.
8. **Ash Wraith (enemy, 1:1)** - A thin ghost made of drifting ash and faint red sparks, centered on a plain dark background.
9. **Cinder Hound (flashback enemy, 1:1)** - A lean hound of charred black stone with glowing orange cracks, centered on a plain dark background.
10. **Cinderheart (weapon, 1:1)** - A heavy broadblade with a blackened blade and a dull orange glow in the core of the guard, centered on a plain dark background.

## Node icons (five separate 1:1 images, flat, plain dark background)

11. Fight: crossed blades. 12. Event: a hooded lantern. 13. Rest: a still water drop. 14. Flashback: a cracked hourglass. 15. Boss: a flaming crown.

## Where the files go

Name them `backdrop.png`, `shrine.png`, `pool.png`, `stranger.png`, `flashback.png`, `bren-pov.png`, `boss.png`, `ash-wraith.png`, `cinder-hound.png`, `cinderheart.png`, and `icon-fight.png`, `icon-event.png`, `icon-rest.png`, `icon-flashback.png`, `icon-boss.png` under `assets/story/ch1/`.
